VISUAL STORYTELLING // THE ART OF NOTICING // SURABAYA & KEDIRI

Finding stories in ordinary moments.

Visual storyteller and observer based between Surabaya and Kediri. Tracing quiet human postures, nostalgic digicam textures, and natural outdoor light—where every frame is an intentional composition of light, emotion, and pace.

Working across independent short vignettes, ambient film reels, and documentary stills. Rather than chasing artificial spectacles, the focus is on the art of noticing what is already there—celebrating natural light, controlled imperfection, and the quiet dignity of everyday spaces.
